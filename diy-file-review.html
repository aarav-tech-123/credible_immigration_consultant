The Service

Do It Yourself (DIY) File Review

Some clients prefer to prepare and file their own application. This service lets you do exactly that, while a licensed consultant reviews your complete application and supporting documents before you submit them to the immigration authority: IRCC for Canada, or the Department of Home Affairs for Australia.

What the review covers

  • Forms completed correctly and consistently
  • Document checklist completeness
  • Eligibility red flags
  • Common refusal risks
  • Answers to your questions in a confidential session
